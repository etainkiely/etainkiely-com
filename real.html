A wider dialogue

Knowledge grows in dialogue.

REAL begins with questions rather than predetermined answers. We listen to people, examine evidence, make assumptions visible and allow different sources of knowledge to challenge what we thought we knew.

Self

Reflection, experience, confidence, uncertainty and the knowledge a person already carries.

Others

Peers, families, practitioners and communities who see the question from another position.

Systems

Data, models, AI, instruments and feedback that can reveal patterns — and can also be wrong.

Place

Weather, ecology, materials, history and the local conditions that abstract models can overlook.

What came before

Knowledge carried through generations, culture, craft, story and repeated experience.

Question before declaration.

Socratic inquiry here is shared curiosity, not interrogation: What do you notice? What makes you think that? What would count as evidence? What might another person know that we do not? What would change your mind? What should happen next?

Measurement does not erase experience. Experience does not exempt a claim from scrutiny.

REAL Analytics

Dialogue With Self and System.

In learning analytics, dialogue becomes specific: the system mirrors evidence of a learner’s trajectory while the learner contributes reflection, confidence, intention and context. Neither account is complete on its own.

See

Make learning evidence visible to the learner rather than keeping the richest view behind a staff dashboard.

Reflect

Ask what the learner notices, believes and intends to do — including what the system cannot infer.

Act

Use the dialogue to support agency, equity and informed action rather than prediction for its own sake.

Sometimes the question is already waiting in the pond.
The REAL habit
Question → co-create → try → measure → listen → change → return → share.

Return makes change visible. Share returns knowledge to the people and places that helped create it.
