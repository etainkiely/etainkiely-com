Professional portfolio

Etain Kiely

Senior Lecturer in Mathematics & Statistics at Atlantic Technological University, Galway. My career has developed across three connected strands: teaching and student success, research and innovation, and building the programmes and systems that allow both to work at scale.

The connecting question

How do we help people become capable learners and judges?

Over more than two decades in Sligo and Galway, that question has moved from classroom teaching, through learning analytics and intelligent assessment, into programme design, research supervision, policy, AI governance and community-based experimentation.

Noticing Maths is the public expression of that career arc: mathematics as a way to pay attention, build evidence and exercise judgement.
