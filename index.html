Qualities worth developing

More than getting the answer.

The aim is not simply higher confidence or faster calculation. It is pride in becoming a more attentive, evidence-aware and independent mathematical judge.

Attention

Notice structure, pattern, difference, scale and anomaly.

Evidence

Measure, compare, represent and ask what the data can legitimately support.

Judgement

Check assumptions, uncertainty, AI output and apparently authoritative answers.

Agency

Know what to do next when the first approach does not work.

Research

Can we see these qualities developing?

The research underneath Noticing Maths asks how we can understand learners more richly than through final marks alone — while refusing to turn people into another set of scores.

Before the calculation

Does a learner predict sign, scale, range or graph shape before using a tool?

After difficulty

Can they locate the first invalid step, respond to feedback and choose a useful next action?

When support disappears

Can they transfer the mathematics independently and defend what the evidence supports?
